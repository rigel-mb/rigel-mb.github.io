Method, results & limitations

Protocol. The sample contains 28,118 training, 5,947 validation and 5,935 test events. Under assumed noise and a 50 MeV cell cut, CNN and Transformer specialists are compared with one masked-pretrained encoder fine-tuned separately for energy and position. Reported neural scores average three training runs; they are not ensemble predictions.

Results. Median position error falls from 0.07040 to 0.04643 in stored coordinate units. Position RMSE is 0.93053 after pretraining versus 0.92632 for direct training and 0.92311 for the physical reference. Seven misplaced windows create rare large errors. For energy, quadratic calibration achieves 2.104% mean absolute relative error versus 2.389% for the pretrained approach.

Scope. Simulated single photons, with a simplified detector response. The study does not establish real detector performance, a general foundation model or net training-compute savings.

Data: Maidannyk & Sahin public simulation dataset · CC BY 4.0.

Current analysis & open questions

Published Zoobot representations are evaluated on 1,126 candidates and 1,126 references matched on brightness, apparent size and sky region. A frozen DINOv2 encoder is compared on a shared subset of 118 objects. No model is fine-tuned locally. Queries exclude both the query image and its matched partner.

On the matched sample, catalogued candidates account for 81.4% of neighbours around candidates and 25.3% around references. These results measure alignment with catalogue labels under a constructed 50% candidate share. References are not confirmed non-lenses. Catalogue selection involved a Zoobot-based lens detector, so selection history, image bands and preprocessing remain central questions for further validation.

Experimental scope

Partial MobileNetV2 fine-tuning on a stratified 60/20/20 split, with 5,400 test images. Model selection uses validation macro-F1. One random image split and one seed do not establish generalisation to new geographic regions. Architecture, image resolution and training budget also vary between configurations.
